Retail Business Analytics Report (Power BI)
An interactive 3-page Power BI dashboard that analyses a global retail business across sales, profit and customer demographics, covering 2011–2014 data from multiple countries and regions.

Objective
To help business stakeholders quickly understand:

How sales and profit are performing compared with last year
Which categories, segments, products and countries drive revenue
Who the most active and most valuable customers are
Which regions and states generate the most value

Key Insights
Sales

Sales grew 51.54% year over year, from $8.34M to $12.64M.
Consumer is the largest segment (51.48% of sales), followed by Corporate (30.25%) and Home Office (18.27%).
Technology leads categories at $4.74M, ahead of Furniture ($4.11M) and Office Supplies ($3.79M).
The United States is the top market ($2.3M), followed by Australia ($0.93M) and France ($0.86M).
Profit

Profit grew 52.34% year over year to $1.47M.
Technology is the most profitable category ($0.66M); Furniture is the lowest ($0.29M) despite high sales, which suggests a margin problem.
The top profit-making product is the Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier (about $25K).
Customers and Regions

Central is the strongest region by sales ($2.8M), followed by South ($1.6M) and North ($1.2M).
The top customer by order count is Bill Eplett (411 orders); the most valuable customer is Tom Ashbrook (about $40K).
Medium priority orders generate most of the profit (58.89%).
England, California and New York are the top 3 most valuable states.
Features
Page navigation buttons (Sales / Profit / Demographics)
Slicers for Country, Year/Month and Top N
Current year vs last year comparison for sales and profit
Donut, bar, treemap, ribbon and area charts
